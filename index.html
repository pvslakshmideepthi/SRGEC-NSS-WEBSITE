Introduction

SRGEC NSS Unit

The NSS Unit of Seshadri Rao Gudlavalleru Engineering College builds student leadership through organized service programs, campus initiatives, outreach activities, and district-level participation.

The unit operates under the guidance of the NSS Programme Officer, faculty coordinators, and student coordinators. Its work includes community development, clean and green programs, awareness campaigns, national camp representation, and cultural discipline through the NSS Band Team.

  • NSS volunteer registration is open for the 2026-2027 academic year.
  • Band team practice runs every Wednesday and Saturday at 4:30 PM.
  • Tree plantation drive scheduled for the first week of September.

Unit profile, achievements, and awards

Started in SRGEC: SRGEC NSS Unit was established to connect engineering education with community service, civic discipline, and leadership development.

Year of establishment: Institution NSS unit records are maintained by the Programme Officer and college NSS cell.

Achievements
