Camps

NSS Camps

National Integration Camps, National Youth Festivals, Adventure Camps, University Camps, State Camps, and Special Camps.

Selection note: Students are selected by the NSS Programme Officer to represent Andhra Pradesh and their university at national-level camps organized by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.


Karnataka

National Integration Camp (NIC)

Mysuru / 12 Jan 2026 - 18 Jan 2026

A national camp focused on unity, cultural exchange, discipline, youth leadership, group discussion, community living, cultural presentation, and inter-state volunteer interaction.

Organizing authority: Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports

Maharashtra

National Youth Festival

Nashik / 12 Jan 2025 - 16 Jan 2025

A youth festival celebrating leadership, culture, innovation, national service, youth dialogue, social awareness exhibitions, and university representation.

Organizing authority: Government of India

Himachal Pradesh

Adventure Camp

Manali / 04 May 2025 - 11 May 2025

Leadership training through trekking, team tasks, first-aid drills, outdoor resilience activities, discipline sessions, and personality development.

Organizing authority: Government of India

Andhra Pradesh

University Special Camp

Krishna District / 20 Dec 2025 - 26 Dec 2025

Seven-day residential camp with rural outreach, sanitation work, education support, health awareness, household surveys, and volunteer leadership responsibilities.

Organizing authority: University NSS Cell

Andhra Pradesh

State NSS Camp

Vijayawada / 06 Aug 2025 - 10 Aug 2025

State-level leadership camp for selected NSS volunteers from affiliated colleges, including parade practice, cultural exchange, NSS reporting, and service planning.

Organizing authority: State NSS Cell

Andhra Pradesh

Special Camp

Adopted Village / 18 Dec 2026 - 24 Dec 2026

Residential camp focused on sanitation, education, village survey, public awareness programs, school visits, clean drinking water awareness, and evening cultural sessions.

Organizing authority: SRGEC NSS Unit

Telangana

Pre-Republic Day Parade Camp

Hyderabad / 01 Nov 2026 - 10 Nov 2026

Selection-oriented camp with parade training, physical fitness, cultural screening, discipline evaluation, and leadership assessment for Republic Day camp consideration.

Organizing authority: Regional Directorate of NSS

Andhra Pradesh

Mega Blood Donation Service Camp

Gudlavalleru / 02 Oct 2026

One-day service camp for blood donor registration, donor awareness, medical coordination, volunteer desk management, and emergency response support.

Organizing authority: SRGEC NSS Unit with District Medical Team
